# Original six-layer vision (historical concept)

The user's initial goal: a minimal-complex ecosystem with few components, each serving a precise purpose; not merely an LLM wrapper but a **Strategic Intelligence Lattice**. The following captures the original conceptual intent without claiming it achieved strategic inevitability.

1. **Core Entity.** One isolated LLM, no plugins or autonomous tools, no raw-world access; abstract reasoning and narrative synthesis only.
2. **Quantum Automata Layer.** The phrase described parallel *classical* plausible-world simulators, not quantum hardware. Each runs forward under one assumption without carrying agent memory.
3. **Collapse Engine.** Compare modeled futures and retain a small set of dominant trajectories, originally framed as pressure gradients rather than probabilities. Its ranking must not itself execute real-world actions.
4. **Strategist Interface.** Give the LLM compressed futures, constraint summaries, adversary-model hypotheses and cost surfaces. It reframes the problem rather than controlling the simulator.
5. **Void Shield.** Persist axioms, identity constants and mission constraints; recompute most other state. The premise that minimal memory always prevents drift was an aspiration, not proven.
6. **Human Administrator Loop.** Human defines objectives, prohibited moves and acceptable losses, and retains final authority; AI supplies maps, reframings and uncertainty analysis.

Original claimed benefits: low attack surface from small components, non-autonomous tools, many model alternatives, LLM isolation, recomputation resilience. These are research/design hypotheses; none demonstrates opponent defeat, global strategic control or a new scientifically validated AI category.
